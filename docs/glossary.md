## Gameplay mechanics / actions
- **Hunting** — Targeting OC / unshielded players
- **Rally** — Club march on a single player
- **Pug scout** — Attacking without scouting to check RSS / defence
- **Node knocking** — Knock players off gather nodes
- **Zeroing** — Kill all troops except the garrison cap
- **Swarming** — Multiple marches on one target
- **Sniping** — Take the final kill on someone else's damage
- **Hive camping** — Leave campus in another club's hive to hit / wait for shields
- **Raid camping** — Camp in another hive to disrupt raid spawns

# Research tree (in-game branches)

Reference for when we redesign our `/research` tree. Each section below is
a canonical branch in the WA research UI plus user-pinned priority notes.

## Summonology
- **Scout / Scout March Speed** — finding targets. Late-game PvP focus.
- **March Capacity** — more troops per march = more damage.
- **Toughness Boost** — reduces injured + dead troops.
- **March Speed Boost** — very valuable, base speed is slow.
- **Strength Boost** — troop damage. *Recommend maxing ASAP.*
- **Ability Boost** — per-troop specialties.

**Tip from user:** only research what's required to unlock the next troop
tier. Maxing this tree is wasteful once T4s are unlocked.

## Academy Management
Initially max everything **except** School Defender March and Rally
Reinforcer March Capacity (those become late-game priorities).

## Tenebral Hunting
*Recommend maxing.* Increases power vs. tenebrals / furies, increases
march capacity, improves energy-potion efficiency. Long and expensive
tree — balance with other goals.

## Resource Gathering
Max to gather the highest nodes for your GH level.

## Elementalism
More important late-game. Skip early; focus elsewhere for faster power
growth. **Stat Boost From Allies** is great for [BFA] bonuses.

## House Unity
Big lever for power. Worth investing in.

## Greater Than The Sum
Everything **except** Shrine Research is important. **Raid Rally Leader
March Capacity** is especially important for leading stronger raid
bosses.

## Concentration
All incredibly useful. **Hero Level Capacity** and **Hero Tier Capacity**
are most important; everything else is also significant.

## Raid Strategy
All very important — **raids are the main source of resources**. Getting
your 2nd / 3rd Concurrent March is huge.

## Wild Focus
Same priorities as Concentration, but for Wild Heroes.
